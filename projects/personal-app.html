01

Overview

Quest is a private birthday app I created for my girlfriend. The three-week experience combined daily minigames with music, personal messages, rewards, and hidden Easter eggs.

02

My contribution

As the sole creator, I defined the game concept, planned the daily experience, and used Base44’s AI development tools to build the app. I also handled independent playtesting before sharing it with its intended user.

03

Experience design

I structured Quest around returning each day, with additional minigames introduced as the experience progressed. Difficulty features added variety, while personalized content connected the games and rewards to the birthday celebration.

04

Outcome

I completed development over one month, from May to June 2026. Quest received positive feedback from my girlfriend, its intended user, and remains private.
