01

Overview

FREEDOM is a 3 lb vertical-spinner combat robot built by Villanova Combat Robotics for competition at the National Havoc Robot League (NHRL). The project brought together mechanical design, component research, fabrication, electrical integration, and competition preparation.

02

My contribution

As team lead and driver, I coordinated mechanical and electrical subsystem development, delegated responsibilities, and managed project deadlines. My work included SolidWorks design iterations, component selection, weight-budget management, fabrication, and system integration.

03

Design & build

Collaborated across drivetrain, chassis, electronics, and rotating-system teams to resolve tradeoffs involving weight, packaging, reliability, and manufacturability within the 3 lb competition constraint.

04

Results & lessons

FREEDOM became one of Villanova’s first 3 lb combat robots to compete at NHRL. I served as its team lead and driver. More detailed competition notes and design iterations will be added here.
