## Featured projects

### [FREEDOM — 3 lb NHRL Combat Robot](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/freedom.html)
Team lead and driver for a nine-member Villanova combat robotics team. My work included SolidWorks design, component research, fabrication, assembly, testing, and competition preparation. I sized a belt-and-pulley system to place the weapon motor inside the robot for better protection.

### [Bread — Class Combat Robot](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/beetlebot.html)
Lead project designer and driver on a three-person team. I designed and wired the control system, helped resolve an underpowered drivetrain, and sourced improved components. Bread reached the class weapons-division championship match.

### [CTI — Fiberglass Roller](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/cti-roller.html)
Identified a repetitive warehouse task and proposed, prototyped, and fabricated a custom roller during my engineering internship. The tool doubled my fiberglass-rolling efficiency and continued to be used after the internship.

### [LimbLet — Wearable Sensor Concept](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/limblet.html)
Collaborated on a three-person team through Villanova’s Engineering Entrepreneurship Summer Institute in Ireland. I worked on the app prototype, logo, marketing, and research into existing wearable and movement-tracking products. LimbLet remains a concept-stage project.

### [Quest — Personalized Game App](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/personal-app.html)
Created a private app in Base44 with five minigames and three weeks of daily progression. I developed a date setting to test scheduled unlocks and refined obstacle-generation requirements to balance variety and playability.

### [Iris+ Drone — Setup & Troubleshooting](https://ethanjprescott-max.github.io/ethanprescott.github.io/projects/iris-drone.html)
An ongoing project involving a 3DR Iris+ quadcopter, Pixhawk flight controller, Mission Planner, radio setup, and telemetry troubleshooting.
